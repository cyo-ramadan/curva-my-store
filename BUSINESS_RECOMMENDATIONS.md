# CURVA MY — Recommended Launch Specification

Status: **working recommendation for validation**, version 10 September 2026.

This file is the operational source of truth for the current website draft. Values may be changed after costing, sampling, fitting, provider approval, and confirmation of the Malaysian fulfilment setup.

## 1. Core positioning

**Category:** Indonesian-inspired baju kurung specifically for Malaysian women who wear XXL and above.

**Primary niche:** the intersection of:

- baju kurung / kebarung appropriate for Malaysian occasions; and
- curve-specific pattern engineering, ready in XXL–4XL with 5XL+ and design customisation by consultation.

**Recommended promise:** `Indonesian-inspired kurung, made for Malaysian curves.`

The differentiation is not decoration alone. It should be visible in the pattern, arm comfort, seated comfort, exact garment measurements, and a custom service that records the specification before production.

## 2. Assumption that changes everything

The recommendations below assume stock is fulfilled **from West Malaysia**. If stock ships directly from Indonesia, do not use the current rates or ETA. Recalculate international freight, customs/tax exposure, returns address, settlement flow, and realistic delivery time first.

### Pricing guardrail

Do not approve these prices until each SKU passes:

`gross margin = (selling price - product cost - packaging - payment fee - shipping subsidy) / selling price`

Recommended pre-ad target: **at least 55% gross margin**. Maximum ad acquisition cost must be calculated from the remaining contribution, not guessed from competitor pricing.

## 5. Initial stock recommendation

Start with one colour per style and validate demand before multiplying variants:

`3 styles × 3 sizes × 2 units = 18 ready pieces`

This is a test inventory plan, not a claim of current stock. Keep 5XL+ made-to-order until fit and demand data justify ready stock.

## 8. Recommended payment flow

1. Customer selects product/size or discusses custom requirements on WhatsApp.
2. CURVA sends a written order summary: item, size/specification, price, shipping, total and timeline.
3. Ready order: full payment.
4. Custom order: 50% deposit; balance before shipping.
5. Send receipt/order reference and update status manually at launch.

Recommended zero-subscription starting provider: **Billplz Basic**, subject to merchant approval and a valid Malaysian settlement account. Enable FPX and DuitNow only after the provider account is approved. Manual bank transfer is a temporary fallback only when CURVA has an appropriate verified account.

Never publish personal banking details in the repository.

## 9. Recommended customer policy

### Ready-size exchange

- Request within 7 calendar days after receipt.
- Item unworn, unwashed, unaltered, with tags attached.
- Customer bears return and replacement shipping for wrong-size choice or change of mind.
- Exchange depends on available stock.

### Wrong or defective item

- Report within 48 hours with opening/defect photo or video.
- CURVA bears replacement shipping after verification.
- If replacement is unavailable, refund the confirmed affected item through an agreed method.

### Custom orders

- No change-of-mind return for approved custom specifications or customer-supplied measurements.
- Production errors remain CURVA's responsibility.
- Deposit refundable only before material procurement or pattern work begins.
- Preserve applicable Malaysian statutory consumer rights.

Get this policy reviewed after the legal business identity and operation country are confirmed.

## 10. Photography and concept renders

Every current fashion image is labelled **Concept render**. It cannot be used as proof of a finished product.

Minimum real-photo set for each launch SKU:

1. full front and back on a curve model;
2. walking and seated fit;
3. arm-raised movement test;
4. bust, bicep, waist and hip close-up;
5. fabric texture and transparency in daylight;
6. payet macro plus inside finishing;
7. measuring tape on actual garment at LD and upper arm;
8. outdoor wedding setting with shallow depth of field, while keeping garment colour accurate.

Keep the unusual graphic language: oversized `CURVE` typography, tailor tools, measuring tape, pattern marks and odd payet names. Product accuracy still has priority over visual drama.

## 12. Meta Ads readiness

Current site tracks WhatsApp clicks to `dataLayer` and emits `WhatsAppClick` when Meta Pixel is installed. Still required:

1. Meta Business Manager and ad account;
2. verified domain or chosen GitHub Pages URL;
3. Meta Pixel ID and privacy disclosure;
4. real product sample photography;
5. confirmed pricing, stock and margin;
6. three or more real proof assets;
7. final legal/business details and active payment flow.

Suggested first test after readiness: one landing page, one hero product, 3–5 creatives, RM30–50/day for 7 days. Treat this as a learning budget. Stop or revise creatives based on landing sessions, WhatsApp click rate, qualified-chat rate and confirmed-order cost.

Recommended UTM pattern:

`?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=kurung_curve_launch&utm_content={creative_name}`

## 14. Launch gate

Do not switch the website from `Pre-launch preview` to a sales-ready claim until:

- samples match the size chart;
- costs support the margin;
- fulfilment origin and shipping are tested;
- payment settles successfully;
- the legal identity and policy contact are published;
- concept renders are replaced by real product photos;
- one complete test order succeeds from WhatsApp to delivery update.
